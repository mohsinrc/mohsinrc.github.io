I am pursuing a PhD in Physics advised by [Ivan Deutsch](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=TiJ3xXcAAAAJ&hl=en) and co-advised by [Niklas Mueller](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DlYqpI8AAAAJ&hl=en) at the [Center for Quantum Information and Control (CQuIC)](https://cquic.unm.edu/). My current research focuses on understanding how noise impacts quantum systems. I am working on quantum-to-classical transitions of quantum systems, both in the physical sense (regression to classical dynamics), and in computational (regression to efficient classical computation). I am also working on creating robust and scalable benchmarks for noisy quantum computation. I am broadly interested in various aspects quantum simulation, open quantum systems and quantum resources (entanglement and magic). 

Previously, I completed a Bachelor's of Science in Physics at [Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)](https://sbasse.lums.edu.pk/department-of-physics) advised by [Adam Zaman Chaudhry](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RNBPLXwAAAAJ&hl=en).

I can be contacted at mraza98@unm.edu.
